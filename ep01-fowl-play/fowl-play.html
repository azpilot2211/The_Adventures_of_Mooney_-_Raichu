Fowl Play

A seagull named Gerald. Four traps. One nap.

A herring gull robs the deck railing every morning at the same hour. Raichu declares war. Mooney declines to participate — and every trap Raichu builds lands on Mooney instead of the bird.

Production notes

What broke last time

Episode 1's original 26 clips ran generate_audio: false and their prompts said “DO NOT render words or mouth-sync dialogue.” That is why nothing had sound and no mouths moved. Both are reversed throughout this script.

One line, one shot

28 lines across 46 shots, almost never stacked. The old cut put three lines into a single eight-second shot where neither cat opened its mouth. Whoever speaks is on screen and framed for it.

Rhythm is free

Cost is 3.5 credits per second regardless of shot count, so cuts cost nothing. Shots here run 5–12s instead of the old uniform 8.04s. Seedance's floor is 4s; the montage beats are generated at 4s and trimmed in the edit.

Voice consistency

Native Seedance voices, so all 46 prompts carry identical wording: Mooney deep, slow, dry; Raichu bright and fast. Across independent generations this is a mitigation, not a guarantee — it's the most likely thing to force retries.

Character lock

Every shot references the existing character sheet 3c223738, the same image behind all 26 original clips. Gerald is new and will need a reference pass of his own.

Text is never generated

Titles, copyright, credit and teaser card are all composited after the fact. Video models garble lettering; the original prompts avoided text everywhere for exactly this reason.
