 Chonk
 A diet. A movement. A seagull with a paper bag.
 Mooney is put on a diet. Raichu is appointed diet buddy and becomes a zealot — converting the neighbourhood, building a movement, printing merch. Mooney starts buying contraband sandwiches from a seagull. Everything collapses. Nobody learns anything useful.

 What's different from Fowl Play
 Mooney wants something. Last time he was passive for five straight minutes — a trait, not an engine. Here he's the addict and the schemer, and Raichu is the obstacle. The roles fully invert.
 Every line turns. "Seagulls live on the ocean" resolved exactly where you expected. "Then I want the bowl" doesn't.
 Shots average 5.2s, not 6.4s. Family Guy and South Park both cut fast. Sixty shots instead of forty-six, each doing less.
 Seven cutaways — and they're continuity-proof. The jump is the joke, so the pipeline's worst weakness becomes a feature.
 It's about something. The South Park spine: this is wellness culture. Trivial premise escalated to institutional absurdity, closing on a completely wrong moral.
 Gerald has a job. He returns as a black-market food dealer instead of just stealing sandwiches again.

 Production notes
 Cutaways solve continuity
 Episode 1's worst flaw was characters teleporting between shots in one continuous scene. Seven of these sixty shots are deliberate hard jumps to unrelated scenes — the discontinuity is the joke, so the pipeline's weakness stops mattering.
 Chain the conversations
 Where shots are continuous — A-1 to A-12, C-3 to C-9, D-1 to D-6 — feed each shot the previous shot's final frame as a reference. Proven on Fowl Play: staging held exactly.
 New characters needed
 GARY the raccoon needs a model sheet. Michael and Lyndie already have theirs and finally speak. The six neighbourhood cats should be one reference image used for every crowd shot.
 Two silences do the work
 B-9 and B-11 are the same shot of six blank cat faces, held far too long, twice. That repetition is the joke. Do not trim them in the edit.
 Play the ending straight
 E-1 to E-5 must look and sound like a genuinely sincere cartoon ending — warm light, swelling strings, no wink. The comedy only works if the presentation is completely earnest.
 Reuse is free
 Main title and end card carry over from Fowl Play at zero cost. Only the episode line and teaser text change, and both are composited in post.
