--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
H. WHAT TO DO INSTEAD OF OBSESSING OVER TAGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Thumbnail first. It is the single biggest lever. Test yours at 210px wide —
   if the word is not instantly readable at that size, it is not finished.
2. Front-load the title with the strange concrete event, not the series name.
3. First two lines of the description carry the hook; everything after
   "...more" is for the algorithm and the committed viewer.
4. Upload on a consistent day. "New episodes every week" is a promise the
   channel description makes — keep it and returning-viewer signals compound.
5. A pinned comment asking a specific question, posted at publish time.
6. Playlists in release order, so one view becomes a session.
