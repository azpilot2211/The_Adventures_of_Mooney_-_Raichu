7. TWO SETTINGS THAT ARE EASY TO GET WRONG
================================================================================

  ALTERED / SYNTHETIC CONTENT DISCLOSURE
    These episodes are generated with AI video tools. YouTube requires you to
    disclose realistic synthetic content. This is stylised 3D cartoon animation
    with talking animals, which no viewer would mistake for real footage, so the
    disclosure is generally NOT required. But YouTube's own guidance is that if
    you are unsure, disclose. Ticking it does not suppress your video. Read the
    current wording in the upload flow and decide per episode.

  MADE FOR KIDS — answer NO
    It looks like a kids' cartoon and is not one. The tone is South Park's
    satirical spine at a PG ceiling, and the closing moral of Episode 2 is
    deliberately monstrous. Marking it "Made for Kids" disables comments, end
    screens, notifications and the subscribe element — that is, everything in
    this document. Answer No, and consider setting an age rating if a future
    episode earns one.

8. TITLE FORMULA FOR FUTURE EPISODES
================================================================================
    [Concrete, strange thing that happens] | The Adventures of Mooney & Raichu | Ep N "Title"

  Lead with the event, never with the series name — the series name means
  nothing to someone who has not watched it yet, and it eats the ~60 characters
  that show on mobile. Keep the episode number, it signals a series worth
  starting.

  Good:  "A Raccoon Moved In And Nobody Has Addressed It | ... | Ep 3"
  Bad:   "The Adventures of Mooney & Raichu Episode 3 — Gary Moves In"
